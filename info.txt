
This app intends to solve the issue of forgetting what to do throughout the day, some people do own agendas to do all of this but some just don't want to keep a extra book to write this all down. A lot of college students own some form of electronic device and since a lot of the population is online as well, a online to do list works great for keeping track of tasks to do. The intended users would be for students. The actions would be signing up to make an account to make the to do lists. It would also give an option to make a new list(options to write the date when the list is due). The list would give options to add items on specific times of the day. When the due date would be nearing the list would have a red glowing dot.
